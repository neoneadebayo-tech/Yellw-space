about.

Yellw is an R&B singer and songwriter from Rivers State, Nigeria. His journey began with writing poetry and sharing song demos recorded on his phone via platforms like Audiomack and SoundCloud. In 2019, he officially launched his music career with the release of his debut single, "Tell Me", across all major streaming platforms.

Yellw's music blends soulful melodies with standout beats and unconventional lyrics, offering an honest exploration of human emotions. His sound is influenced by artists like Frank Ocean, PartyNextDoor, Russ, SiR, and Daniel Caesar.

As an independent artist, Yellw has performed on prestigious stages, including TEDx, and released an EP that has amassed over 50,000 streams across all platforms. Through his singles, he tells compelling stories of love, loss, self-care, and personal growth, solidifying his place in the evolving R&B scene.
